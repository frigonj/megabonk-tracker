# Megabonk Wiki Reference — Maps

Source: https://megabonk.wiki/wiki/Maps, https://megabonk.wiki/wiki/Forest, https://megabonk.wiki/wiki/Desert, https://megabonk.wiki/wiki/Graveyard (community wiki, MediaWiki)
Snapshot date: 2026-08-17

This is a point-in-time snapshot of community-maintained wiki content. It is **not official game documentation** and may drift from actual current game behavior/balance as patches are released. Use as a reference/hint source only — verify against live event data where possible.

## Forest

- **Biome description:** "A green place full of trees, birds and great vibes. Also everything will try to kill you." Lush woodland aesthetic; trees, rocks, medieval structures create collision/visibility obstacles.
- **Unlock:** Available by default (starting map).
- **Tiers:** 3.
- **Role:** Introductory map/"gateway biome" for learning movement mechanics and early upgrades. Early waves are simple goblins clearable with starting weapons.
- **Progression:** Each stage ends in a boss fight; defeating the boss spawns a teleporter/portal to advance (tier-dependent).
- **Mini-bosses (spawn once per run, Tiers 1-3):** Chunkham The Terrible, Stone Golem. Spawn timing: 7:00 or 2:00 remaining (6:30 or 1:30 remaining for Tier 3).
- **Final bosses:** Lil Bark (Tier 1), Chadbark (Tier 2), Bark Vader (Tier 3 — see bosses.md for full fight breakdown).
- **Special mechanic:** Reaching Forest Tier 2 Stage 2 while playing CL4NK unlocks the Desert map for future runs.

## Desert

- **Biome description:** "A hot, dry and dusty place." Open terrain, sandy ground, sparse vegetation — cactus-like plants, cracked earth, dust clouds, tumbleweeds, ruins, distant mesas/rock formations.
- **Unlock:** Reach Forest Tier 2, Stage 2 while playing CL4NK.
- **Tiers:** 3. Sequential unlock: completing Tier 1 Stage 1 unlocks Tier 2; completing Tier 2 Stage 2 unlocks Tier 3.
- **Difficulty:** Tougher enemies, more aggressive spawns; better visibility overall but more exposure/less cover.
- **Sandstorm event:** Recurring environmental hazard — reduces visibility to near-zero briefly, obscuring enemies until very close. At least one Sandstorm is guaranteed per run. Fully charging a Charge Shrine during an active Sandstorm counts toward the Tornado weapon's unlock quest.
- **Mini-bosses (Tiers 1-2):** Sand Golem, Scorpionussy. Same spawn timing pattern as Forest (7:00/2:00, or 6:30/1:30 at Tier 3).
- **Final bosses:** Anubis (Tier 1), Anubruh (Tier 2), Juge Anubis (Tier 3).

## Graveyard

- **Biome description:** "The home of ghosts and other spooks."
- **Unlock:** As Calcium, on Desert Tier 2, defeat the undead boss.
- **Tiers:** 1 (unique format — 16-minute run length instead of the standard ~10 minutes; 2 stages instead of the usual tier/stage structure).
- **Mini-bosses:** Alternates between Calcium's Dad and Ghostham the Dead at 13, 9, and 3 minutes remaining. Spawn order/rotation depends on which is encountered first.
- **Bosses (manually spawned, not on a fixed timer):** Jeff, Mike, Ted, Gary, Spooky Steve (appears in the first and second crypt).
- **Notable feature:** Spooky Steve's spawn is tied to crypt progression rather than a timed interval like the other mini-bosses. No additional unlock requirements, biome hazards, or environmental mechanics were documented by the wiki as of this snapshot.
