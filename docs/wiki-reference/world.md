## Overview

Megabonk's world is structured as a set of distinct maps, each with a tiered progression system and themed biome. As of this snapshot, three maps exist: **Forest**, **Desert**, and **Graveyard**. The wiki's page author noted plans to document individual maps in more depth over time, anticipating additional maps being added to the game in future updates.

## Maps Summary

### Forest
"A green place full of trees, birds and great vibes. Also everything will try to kill you." Introductory map with lush woodland aesthetics and relatively low early difficulty; teaches core mechanics via simple enemies (goblins).
- **Mini-bosses:** Chunkham The Terrible, Stone Golem
- **Bosses:** Lil Bark (Tier 1), Chadbark (Tier 2), Bark Vader (Tier 3)
- **Unlocked by default.**

### Desert
"A hot, dry and dusty place." More open terrain, increased difficulty, sandstorms that temporarily reduce visibility.
- **Mini-bosses:** Sand Golem, Scorpionussy
- **Bosses:** Anubis (Tier 1), Anubruh (Tier 2), Juge Anubis (Tier 3)
- **Unlock:** Reach Stage 2 of Forest Tier 2 while playing CL4NK (teleporting to the second stage unlocks it).

### Graveyard
"The home of ghosts and other spooks." Unique 16-minute format with 2 stages instead of the standard 3-tier structure.
- **Mini-bosses:** alternates between Calcium's Dad and Ghostham the Dead.
- **Bosses (manually spawned):** Jeff, Mike, Ted, Gary, Spooky Steve.
- **Unlock:** As Calcium, on Desert Tier 2, defeat the undead boss.

See `maps.md` for full per-map detail including spawn timings and tier structure.
