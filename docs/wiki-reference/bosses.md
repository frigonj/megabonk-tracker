## Bark Vader (Forest Tier 3 Final Boss) — Full Detail

Described by the wiki as "one of the hardest encounters in the game."

### Phase Structure
Two alternating phases:
- **DPS Phase** — direct damage dealing against the boss.
- **Pylon Phase** — triggers at every 25% HP threshold. Boss becomes immune and gradually heals; players must charge three pylons to restore vulnerability.

### Phase Escalation
After each completed Pylon Phase:
- One additional weapon slot unlocks for the boss (left to right, i.e., the boss uses more attack types over time)
- Orbs spawn more frequently
- Boss gains Armor
- Boss gains Attack Damage

### Attacks

**Unavoidable / environmental:**
- Zone damage — chip damage (~2/sec) in certain areas.

**Dodgeable:**
- Trailing roots — AoE zones that follow the player.
- Tracking roots — single large AoE that predicts player position.
- Cone roots — three lines radiating from the boss.
- Blue orbs — high-speed, inflicts Freeze on hit.
- Red orbs — tracking, inflicts a debuff blocking healing/regeneration for 5 seconds (see stats.md Conditions section — "Red Condition"). Wiki flags this as the **highest priority** to dodge/clear.
- Yellow orbs — high-speed arcing pattern, inflicts Poison for 5 seconds.
- Spawned adds — low-HP minions.

### Strategic Notes (per wiki)
- Prioritize survival over offense given the boss's health pool and invulnerability windows during Pylon Phase.
- Weapon-lockout mechanics (boss withholds weapon types until later phases) disadvantage certain characters, specifically **Amog** and **Vlad**.
- Consider non-weapon damage sources during lockout: Moldy Cheese, Cursed Doll, Thunder Mitts.
- Shield-based defense (Aegis) is recommended.
- Regeneration/shields are essential during Pylon Phases since **Lifesteal does not function** during that phase.
- "All attacks are dodgeable with constant movement."
- Clear red and blue orbs before attempting to charge pylons.
- Aim for 3-4 jumps (via jump-height modifiers) to reach pylons quickly.

### Patch history affecting this fight (see patch-notes.md for full detail)
- **v1.0.4:** Boss returns one weapon per phase (starter weapon only needed for phase 1); pylon zones enlarged; reduced boss healing; orb damage reduced; blue-orb freeze duration reduced from 3s to 1s; orb cooldown increased (less frequent spawns); weapon range increased during the fight.
