# Megabonk Wiki Reference — Weapons

Source: https://megabonk.wiki/wiki/Weapons (community wiki, MediaWiki)
Snapshot date: 2026-08-17

This is a point-in-time snapshot of community-maintained wiki content. It is **not official game documentation** and may drift from actual current game behavior/balance as patches are released. Use as a reference/hint source only — verify against live event data where possible.

## Index Overview

29 unique weapons total: 7 starter weapons available from the beginning, and 22 unlockable weapons requiring specific conditions. Unlocked weapons are purchased with Silver from the Unlocks tab in the Main Menu, after which they enter the loot pool for future runs.

All weapons follow a five-tier rarity upgrade scale: **Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary**, each with per-stat numeric values shown below where the wiki provided them.

## Starter Weapons (7)

### Sword
- **Description:** Slashes through multiple enemies in a wide sweeping arc, hitting multiple targets.
- **Default on:** Sir Oofie
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Knockback 0.5/0.6/0.7/0.8/1; Size 20%/24%/28%/32%/40%
- **Tips:** Prioritize Damage, Projectile Count, Size. Knockback is defensive utility only (no damage/hit-count benefit).
- **Leads to:** Axe (kill 2,000 enemies with Sword); Dexecutioner (kill 12,500 enemies with Sword)

### Flamewalker
- **Description:** Leaves a trail of fire behind you; continuous AoE damage to enemies passing through.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Duration 0.18s/0.21s/0.25s/0.29s/0.36s; Size 15%/18%/21%/24%/30%
- **Tips:** Kite enemies through fire trails. Synergizes with Aura and other AoE weapons for stacked DoT. Good early-game passive farming.
- **Leads to:** Dragonfire item (4,000 fire-damage kills)

### Lightning Staff
- **Description:** Summons lightning to smite nearby enemies; instant AoE lightning damage.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Bounces 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Size 20%/24%/28%/32%/40%
- **Tips:** Size Tome extends strike range; Quantity Tome multiplies simultaneous strikes; Bounces chain strikes between enemies — key to crowd control scaling.
- **Leads to:** Wireless Dagger (reach level 15 with Lightning Staff); Lightning Orb item (3,000 total lightning damage)

### Firestaff
- **Description:** Shoots fireballs that explode on impact; AoE fire damage.
- **Default on:** Fox
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2.5/3/3.5/4/5; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 0.1/0.12/0.14/0.16/0.2; Size 16%/19%/22%/26%/32%
- **Tips:** Size/Quantity tomes for crowd damage; Projectile Speed lowest priority. Pairs with Dragonfire for burn DoT.
- **Leads to:** Dragonfire item (4,000 fire-damage kills)

### Chunkers
- **Description:** Summons chunky rocks that orbit the player, dealing damage/knockback, slowing as they drift outward before resetting.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 3/3.6/4.2/4.8/6; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 0.35/0.42/0.49/0.56/0.7; Knockback 0.7/0.8/0.8/1/1.3; Size 20%/24%/28%/32%/40%
- **Tips:** Size Tome for coverage; Quantity Tome to close gaps. Strong defensive "enemy blender" when upgraded.

### Bone
- **Description:** Throws bones that bounce off enemies; single-target-ish projectile with bounce.
- **Default on:** Calcium
- **Starts with:** 1 level of bounce (hits 2 enemies immediately)
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2.5/3/3.5/4/5; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Bounces 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 0.2/0.24/0.28/0.32/0.4; Crit Chance and Crit Damage upgrades are 0 across all tiers (not available on this weapon)
- **Tips:** Projectile Speed Tome accelerates travel through groups; Quantity Tome increases bones fired per cycle (sequential launch).

### Bow
- **Description:** Shoots arrows that pierce through enemies.
- **Default on:** Robinette
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 1.75/2.1/2.45/2.8/3.5; Crit Chance 8%/10%/11%/13%/16%; Crit Damage 18%/22%/25%/29%/36%; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 0.3/0.36/0.42/0.48/0.6; Size 0.2/0.2/0.2/0.3/0.3
- **Tips:** Size/Quantity tomes for spread coverage; Cooldown Tome pairs with piercing. Projectile Count fires in a horizontal cone. Projectile Speed lowest priority.

## Unlockable Weapons (22)

### Revolver
- **Description:** Fires multiple bullets; crowd control via multi-projectile and bounce.
- **Unlock:** Defeat 7,500 enemies cumulatively, then 1 Silver from Unlocks.
- **Default on:** CL4NK
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2.5/3/3.5/4/5; Crit Chance 10%/12%/14%/16%/20%; Crit Damage 20%/24%/28%/32%/40%; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Bounces 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 0.4/0.5/0.6/0.6/0.8
- **Tips:** Prioritize Bounces for exponential chain damage; balance with base Damage for bosses. Precision Tome, Quantity Tome, and CL4NK's crit passive synergize.

### Aegis
- **Description:** Blocks incoming attacks and sends out shockwaves; damages attackers when you take damage. Defensive/offensive hybrid, lower direct damage.
- **Default on:** Sir Oofie (thematically)
- **Unlock:** Block 500 damage using Armor as Sir Oofie (requires Armor Tome unlocked first via 5,000 enemy kills as Sir Oofie), then 1 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Knockback 0.8/0.9/1.1/1.2/1.5; Size 15%/18%/21%/24%/30%
- **Tips:** Scales with Armor investment; Quantity Tome increases max shields; Thorns Tome/Spiky Shield boost defense; Cooldown Tome speeds shield recovery. Best on Athena and Sir Oofie.

### Bananarang
- **Description:** Throws bananas that return to the owner, hitting on both outward and return paths.
- **Default on:** Monke
- **Unlock:** Find a hidden banana in the Forest (commonly Stage 1), then 1 Silver. Required to free Monke.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 0.1/0.12/0.14/0.16/0.2; Size 14%/17%/20%/22%/28%
- **Tips:** Strong in corridors/dense waves due to dual-hit. Prioritize Projectile Speed for travel distance.

### Aura
- **Description:** Damages enemies in an area around you; consistent close-range AoE.
- **Default on:** Megachad
- **Unlock:** Survive 2 consecutive minutes without taking damage in a single run (does not accumulate across runs; easiest on Forest Stage 1), then 1 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 1.4/1.7/2/2.2/2.8; Size 14%/17%/20%/22%/28% (smallest upgrade pool in the game — no other stats apply)
- **Tips:** Attack Speed raises tick rate. Prioritize Movement Speed items for positioning. Works against wall-climbing enemies. Best with Megachad (Flex CC) and Noelle (Size scaling).

### Axe
- **Description:** Throws spinning axes dealing area damage; area-denial tool.
- **Default on:** Ogre
- **Unlock:** Defeat 2,000 enemies with Sword (Sir Oofie recommended), then 1 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 1.5/1.8/2.1/2.4/3; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Duration 0.08s/0.1s/0.11s/0.13s/0.16s; Size 10%/12%/14%/16%/20%
- **Tips:** Use to block enemy passage/safe zones. Prioritize Duration. Excellent with Noelle (free size scaling frees upgrade slots for damage).

### Space Noodle
- **Description:** Attaches a beam to a nearby enemy, damaging anything that touches the beam. Attached enemy cannot be killed until the beam's duration ends (it can still act normally).
- **Unlock:** Complete Desert Tier 2 as Tony McZoom. Reward: 2 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Duration 0.2s/0.24s/0.28s/0.32s/0.4s; Size 20%/24%/28%/32%/40% (no Projectile Count benefit)
- **Tips:** Size Tome widens beam; Duration Tome extends it (may be counterproductive since target survives longer). Considered weak for late-game swarms due to the un-killable-target mechanic.

### Sniper Rifle
- **Description:** High-powered, manually-aimed piercing bullet.
- **Default on:** Bush
- **Unlock:** Upgrade Precision Tome to Level 10 in a run, then 2 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 4/4.8/5.6/6.4/8; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Size 13%/16%/18%/21%/26% (no direct Crit Chance upgrade — must come from other sources)
- **Tips:** Line up enemies for piercing shots. Attack Speed reduces shot delay. Size widens bullet hitbox. Excellent with Bush's marking passive.

### Slutty Rocket
- **Description:** Fires homing missiles that auto-seek enemies (fire-and-forget).
- **Unlock:** Unlock CL4NK (defeat Forest Tier 1 boss), then 15,000 kills as CL4NK, then 2 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Crit Chance 8%/10%/11%/13%/16%; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 0.2/0.24/0.28/0.32/0.4
- **Tips:** Prioritize Projectile Count/Speed over Damage since missiles self-aim. Quantity Tome, Bush (crit damage/level), Movement Speed items all synergize.

### Shotgun
- **Description:** Manually-aimed spread shot, stronger at close range, pellets pierce; damage falls off with distance.
- **Unlock:** 5% drop from breaking a Tumbleweed on Desert Stage 2.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 3/3.6/4.2/4.8/6; Crit Chance 7%/8%/10%/11%/14%; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Crit Damage 0 across all tiers (not available); Size 15%/18%/21%/24%/30%
- **Tips:** Projectile Count increases pellets/shot (not fire rate). Size reduces range penalty. Best on Noelle, Bandit, Bush.

### Mines
- **Description:** Deploys stationary mines that explode when enemies approach; notable knockback.
- **Unlock:** Kill 7,500 enemies with Slutty Rocket. Reward: 2 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 3/3.6/4.2/4.8/6; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Duration 0.4s/0.48s/0.56s/0.64s/0.8s; Size 15%/18%/21%/24%/30%
- **Tips:** Size Tome for blast radius; Quantity Tome for more mines/cycle; Cooldown Tome for faster placement.

### Wireless Dagger
- **Description:** Throws homing "smart daggers" — near-guaranteed hits.
- **Default on:** Tony McZoom
- **Unlock:** Upgrade Lightning Staff to Level 15, then 2 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Bounces 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 0.1/0.12/0.14/0.16/0.2
- **Tips:** Prioritize Bounces + Projectile Count for swarms; balance with Damage for kill speed.

### Frostwalker
- **Description:** AoE freeze + damage around the player. Ice damage freezes enemies, setting up follow-up hits.
- **Default on:** Noelle
- **Unlock:** Find the Ice Cube (Legendary item), freeze 1,000 cumulative enemies with it, then 2 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Duration 0.12s/0.14s/0.17s/0.19s/0.24s; Size 10%/12%/14%/16%/20%
- **Tips:** Utility/lockdown role, not primary damage. Pair with high-damage AoE (Axe, Black Hole). Attack Speed increases freeze frequency. Cursed Doll extends the setup window. Excellent on Noelle.

### Tornado
- **Description:** Sends out piercing, knockback tornadoes. Projectile/Crowd Control/Knockback weapon.
- **Default on:** Birdo
- **Unlock:** Charge 5 Charge Shrines during a Sandstorm event on any Desert stage, then 2 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 4/4.8/5.6/6.4/8; Knockback 0.6/0.72/0.84/0.96/1.2; Size 14%/17%/20%/22%/28%
- **Tips:** Defensive control tool, not primary damage. Pair with Sniper Rifle/Shotgun/Dragon's Breath for spacing. Prioritize Knockback, Projectile Count, Size.

### Dexecutioner
- **Description:** Piercing blade projectile with a small (2%) chance to instantly execute an enemy.
- **Default on:** Bandit
- **Unlock:** Kill 12,500 enemies with Sword.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Crit Chance 5%/6%/7%/8%/10%; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Size 20%/24%/28%/32%/40%
- **Tips:** Joe's Dagger (+1% damage per execute), Size/Quantity/Cooldown Tomes increase execute opportunities. Black Hole groups targets. Overpowered Lamp doubles execute trigger. Best on Bandit, Noelle.
- **Leads to:** Joe's Dagger item (10,000 kills with Dexecutioner)

### Blood Magic
- **Description:** AoE/Instant/Utility weapon. 5% chance on kill to permanently gain +1 Max HP (uncapped).
- **Default on:** Vlad
- **Unlock:** Get Bloody Tome (defeat 12,500 enemies), upgrade it to Level 12 in one run, then 2 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 1.5/1.8/2.1/2.4/3; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Size 15%/18%/21%/24%/30%
- **Tips:** Pair with high-damage secondaries (Sniper Rifle, Katana). Beefy Ring (+20% damage per 100 Max HP) scales strongly with this weapon's HP stacking.

### Black Hole
- **Description:** Fires a black hole that pulls enemies in; crowd-control setup tool, not primary damage.
- **Default on:** Spaceman
- **Unlock:** Upgrade Knockback Tome to Level 10, then 2 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 1.3/1.5/1.8/2.0/2.5; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Duration 0.12s/0.14s/0.17s/0.19s/0.24s; Size 13%/16%/18%/21%/26%
- **Tips:** Pairs with high-damage AoE, Unstable Transfusion, Bloody Cleaver (prime high-HP targets), and Dexecutioner (grouped execute targets). Prioritize Duration and Projectile Count.

### Poison Flask
- **Description:** AoE/DoT/Projectile — throws toxic flasks that poison on impact.
- **Default on:** Amog
- **Unlock:** Defeat the Scorpionussy miniboss (Desert) three times.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 0.5/0.6/0.7/0.8/1; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Duration 1s/1.2s/1.4s/1.6s/2s; Size 15%/18%/21%/24%/30%
- **Tips:** Crit Chance/Damage are wasted (poison can't crit); Damage stat scales poorly. Duration/Size/Quantity/Cooldown help. Best on Amog (detonate + spread passive). Wiki assessment: "exceptionally bad" for Final Swarm survival.

### Katana
- **Description:** Melee, single-target — targets the closest enemy.
- **Default on:** Ninja
- **Unlock:** 5% drop from breaking Tumbleweeds on Desert Stage 1, then 2 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2.2/2.6/3.1/3.5/4.4; Crit Chance 8%/10%/11%/13%/16%; Crit Damage 20%/24%/28%/32%/40%; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Size 20%/24%/28%/32%/40%
- **Tips:** Crit-focused build; Precision Tome pairs best. Best on Noelle, Bush, Spaceman (Black Hole grouping).

### Dragon's Breath
(wiki page reachable at both `/wiki/Dragons_Breath` and `/wiki/Dragon%27s_Breath` — same content)
- **Description:** Fires an AoE fire cone in the movement direction. Weak early (small size), strong scaled.
- **Unlock:** Kill 1,000 Wisp enemies as Fox on Desert. Reward: 2 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 3/3.6/4.2/4.8/6; Duration 0.2s/0.24s/0.28s/0.32s/0.4s; Size 15%/18%/21%/24%/30%
- **Tips:** Size + Duration Tomes for range/uptime; Attack Speed shrinks gaps between bursts. Pairs with Dragonfire for burn DoT.

### Dice
- **Description:** Throws dice dealing 1-6 damage; rolling a 6 permanently increases the weapon's own Crit Chance by 0.5% for the run (infinite scaling "snowball" weapon).
- **Default on:** Dicehead
- **Unlock:** Get Luck Tome (0.01% enemy drop chance), upgrade to Level 12 in one run, then 4 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Crit Chance 10%/12%/14%/16%/20%; Crit Damage 20%/24%/28%/32%/40%; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 0.2/0.24/0.28/0.32/0.4; Size 15%/18%/21%/24%/30%
- **Tips priority:** Damage & Projectile Count first, then Crit Damage, Size, Crit Chance, Projectile Speed last. Best on Bush (crit damage/level) and Noelle (free size).

### Hero Sword
- **Description:** Hybrid melee/ranged — slashes fire a piercing ranged projectile too.
- **Unlock:** Defeat any stage boss under "purist" conditions (no ground items/powerups/chests picked up, no shrines except Boss Shrine; level-up choices still allowed). Reward: 4 Silver.
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 4/4.8/5.6/6.4/8; Size 15%/18%/21%/24%/30%
- **Tips:** Prioritize Projectile Count and Size; Projectile Speed maximizes range. Wiki suggests durable starter (Sir Oofie), Quantity Tome over Damage Tome early, Forest for the easiest purist boss run, open areas for mobility.

### Corrupted Sword
- **Description:** Melee + rear ranged slash (rear does half damage, longer range, pierces). HP-based damage multiplier — much stronger at low HP.
- **Default on:** Sir Chadwell
- **Unlock:** Get Cursed Tome (defeat a stage boss in under 5 minutes), then level Cursed Tome to 20 before the in-run timer hits 10:00. Reward: 8 Silver.
- **HP damage scaling:** 100% HP → x0.4; 50% HP → x0.862; 1% HP → x2.213; exactly 1 HP → x3.0
- **Upgrade table:** Damage 2/2.4/2.8/3.2/4; Projectile Count 1/1.2/1.4/1.6/2; Projectile Speed 4/4.8/5.6/6.4/8; Size 15%/18%/21%/24%/30%
- **Tips:** Pair with Shield items, Mirror, Kevin to sustain at low HP. Best on Athena, Noelle.
