# Megabonk Wiki Reference — Items

Source: https://megabonk.wiki/wiki/Items (community wiki, MediaWiki)
Snapshot date: 2026-08-17

This is a point-in-time snapshot of community-maintained wiki content. It is **not official game documentation** and may drift from actual current game behavior/balance as patches are released. Use as a reference/hint source only — verify against live event data where possible.

## Index Overview

**77 items** total per the wiki's index page, with **35 unlocked by default**. Acquired via Treasure Chests, the Shady Guy (random merchant), Quests, or the Unlocks Menu (purchased with Silver after meeting an unlock condition). The wiki's individual item listing actually enumerates 85 page links (some naming/alias overlaps); all 85 individual pages were fetched and are catalogued below by rarity tier as shown on each page.

Several items have "TBD" strategy/effect sections on the source wiki as of this snapshot — noted explicitly below where the source itself was incomplete.

## Default / Starting Items

### Moldy Cheese
- **Rarity:** Default. **Effect:** +40% chance to poison enemies on hit.
- Foundational poison-build item. Synergizes with Gas Mask, Moldy Gloves, high-attack-rate weapons (Black Hole, Tornado, Revolver, Slutty Rocket), and Cursed Doll.

### Borgar
- **Rarity:** Default. **Effect:** +2% chance to spawn a healing Borgar on kill.
- Best early when other healing is scarce; scales with kill frequency (AoE/rapid-clear weapons).

### Clover
- **Rarity:** Default. **Effect:** +7.5% Luck.
- Improves level-up/chest reward rarity; universally useful, no build requirement.

### Time Bracelet
- **Rarity:** Default. **Effect:** +8% XP Gain.
- Front-loads progression; helps establish build before difficulty ramps.

### Slippery Ring
- **Rarity:** Default. **Effect:** +15% Evasion.
- One of few early Evasion sources; diminishing returns as more Evasion is stacked from other sources.

### Golden Glove
- **Rarity:** Default. **Effect:** +15% more gold from kills.
- Economy item; best acquired early for cumulative benefit.

### Oats
- **Rarity:** Default. **Effect:** +25 Max HP.
- Simple flat HP; synergizes with %-Max-HP-scaling effects. (Acquisition method not specified on source page.)

### Medkit
- **Rarity:** Default. **Effect:** +45 HP Regen (~0.75 HP/sec).
- Continuous regen, good vs. chip damage; scales in value with Max HP items. (Acquisition method not specified on source page.)

### Beer
- **Rarity:** Common (listed under default-available). **Effect:** +20% Damage, −5% Max HP.
- Trades survivability for damage; pair with Medkit/Oats to offset HP loss.

### Wrench
- **Rarity:** Common. **Effect:** Charge Shrines 4% faster, +7.5% increased Shrine rewards.
- Additive charge-speed reduction; 25+ copies charge Shrines instantly.

### Golden Shield
- **Rarity:** Common. **Effect:** Gain gold when taking damage.
- "Profit tank" strategy; pairs with Aegis/Spiky Shield.

### Thunder Mitts
- **Rarity:** Common. **Effect:** On hit, generate a lightning strike (10s cooldown, fixed regardless of Attack Speed).
- Synergizes with Lightning Staff, Lightning Orb, Battery.

### Feathers
- **Rarity:** Common. **Effect:** Increased jump height and distance.
- Synergizes with Scarf (airborne damage); helps collect floating pickups.

### Electric Plug
- **Rarity:** Common. **Effect:** On taking damage, emit an electric shock.
- Reactive crowd control; complements Lightning Staff/Thunder Mitts.

### Cowards Cloak
- **Rarity:** Common. **Effect:** +5% base Move Speed, plus a Move Speed boost when hit.
- Escape/reposition tool; synergizes with Thunder Mitts/Electric Plug (rewards being hit).

### Unstable Transfusion
- **Rarity:** Rare. **Effect:** 27% chance to apply "Bloodmark" on hit — delayed damage triggering 5s later (resets on reapplication; fires immediately if it would overkill).
- Pure damage, no healing; scales with Attack Speed/Projectile Count, not crit.

### Backpack
- **Rarity:** Rare. **Effect:** +1 Projectile Count for all weapons.
- Broadly useful; synergizes with Quantity Tome and high-hit-count builds.

### Beacon
- **Rarity:** Rare. **Effect:** Charging a Charge Shrine creates a healing zone (size scales with Size stat) and spawns +2 extra Shrines in future stages.
- Synergizes with Wrench, Golden Shield.

### Phantom Shroud
- **Rarity:** Rare. **Effect:** After evading, next hit deals 2x damage plus brief Attack Speed/Move Speed boost.
- Best on evasion-focused characters (Fox, Athena); pairs with Slippery Ring.

### Campfire
- **Rarity:** Common. **Effect:** Standing still heals you (slow regen while stationary).
- Synergizes with Aura/Frostwalker (stationary playstyles); pairs with Cowards Cloak for repositioning breaks.

### Power Gloves
- **Rarity:** Common. **Effect:** 8% chance to create a giant blast on hit (AoE damage).
- Synergizes with rapid-fire weapons (Firestaff, Bananarang, Aura) and Spicy Meatball.

### Za Warudo
- **Rarity:** Legendary (available by default). **Effect:** On fatal damage, time freezes once to prevent death (one-time save).
- Pairs with Speed Boi, Holy Book. Emergency safety net for bosses/Final Swarm.

### Big Bonk
- **Rarity:** Legendary (available by default). **Effect:** 2% chance to "BONK" an enemy for 20x damage.
- Best with frequent-attack weapons (Aura, Bananarang, Firestaff) and luck items.

### Overpowered Lamp
- **Rarity:** Legendary (available by default). **Effect:** Gives all other on-hit-proc items an extra roll to trigger (can multi-proc from a single hit).
- Compatible with 16 named items: Moldy Cheese, Ice Crystal, Unstable Transfusion, Kevin, Slutty Cannon, Quin's Mask, Eagle Claw, Grandma's Secret Tonic, Cursed Grabbies, Big Bonk, Spicy Meatball, Ice Cube, Power Gloves, Lightning Orb, Dragonfire, Joe's Dagger, Bloody Cleaver. **Does NOT** extra-proc Demonic Blade or Giant Fork despite similar trigger conditions.

### Spiky Shield
- **Rarity:** Epic (available by default). **Effect:** +2 Thorns per 1% Armor, +10% Armor.
- Retaliation-build cornerstone; pairs with Cactus, Quins Mask, Aegis.

### Scarf
- **Rarity:** Epic (available by default). **Effect:** +50% Damage while airborne.
- Pairs with Feathers, Bananarang, Aura, Turbo Socks (maximize airtime).

### Slurp Gloves
- **Rarity:** Epic (available by default). **Effect:** Cast blood magic on hit — heals 7.5% with an AoE burst (9s cooldown).
- Pairs with Unstable Transfusion, Bloody Cleaver, Demonic Blood. Strong in Final Swarm.

### Mirror
- **Rarity:** Epic (available by default). **Effect:** Reflects all incoming damage and grants brief invulnerability (reflect damage scales with incoming damage).
- Strong early defensive; pairs with Aegis, Electric Plug. Scales well vs elites.

### Giant Fork
- **Rarity:** Legendary (available by default). **Effect:** +15% Crit Chance (stacks per copy); crits have a 14% chance to become "Megacrits" (much higher damage).
- Pairs with Forbidden Juice, Grandma's Secret Tonic, Precision Tome, CL4NK. Overpowered Lamp does NOT boost Megacrit chance.

### Golden Ring
- **Rarity:** Legendary (available by default). **Effect:** "It doesn't seem to do anything..." — no documented mechanical effect on the wiki. Possibly a joke/placeholder item or undocumented effect.

## Common Items

### Gym Sauce
- **Effect:** +10% Damage. **Unlock:** available from start.
- Multiplies with most damage effects — better scaling than typical Common items. **Stacks additively (worse) with:** Idle Juice, Brass Knuckles. **Stacks multiplicatively (better) with:** Charge Shrine, Chaos Tome, Beefy Ring, Demonic Soul, Joe's Dagger, Scarf, Gamer Goggles, Credit Card Red, Beer, Eagle Claw, Tactical Glasses, Speed Boi.

### Turbo Socks
- **Effect:** +15% Movement Speed. **Unlock:** Upgrade Agility Tome to Level 5.
- Pairs with Cowards Cloak; supports evasion/shrine-completion playstyles.

### Tactical Glasses
- **Effect:** +20% more damage to enemies above 90% HP. **Unlock:** Defeat a Miniboss in under 25 seconds.
- Best with burst/precision weapons (Sniper Rifle, Dexecutioner, Hero Sword).

### Battery
- **Effect:** +8% Attack Speed. **Unlock:** Upgrade Cooldown Tome to Level 5.
- Synergizes with rapid-fire weapons (Firestaff, Lightning Staff, Bananarang); boosts Aura tick rate and freeze frequency.

### Forbidden Juice
- **Effect:** +10% Crit Chance. **Unlock:** Land 100 critical hits in a single run.
- Pairs with Bow, Revolver, Katana, Demonic Blade, Grandma's Secret Tonic, Bush.

### Cursed Doll
- **Effect:** Curses enemies, dealing 30% of their Max HP as damage per second (ignores armor). **Unlock:** Die 10 times.
- Pairs with Cursed Tome, Corrupted Sword, Bloody Cleaver.

### Boss Buster
- **Effect:** +15% more damage to Elites and Bosses. **Unlock:** Defeat a Stage Boss in under 30 seconds.
- Valuable for tightening boss-kill timers mid/late game.

### Key
- **Effect:** +10% chance to open chests for free (per wiki: actual per-copy chance is 9.1%, computed via `freeChestChance = totalKeyChance / (totalKeyChance + 1)`). **Unlock:** Purchase items from 25 chests.
- **Stacking:** 1 Key = 9.1%, 5 Keys = 33.3%, 10 Keys = 50%. Roll happens after chest contents are determined, so the very first Key can retroactively free that chest. Free-chest triggers don't raise the next chest's cost. Luck stat does NOT affect Key rolls. Pairs with Golden Shield, Kevin, Credit Cards.

### Ice Crystal
- **Effect:** 7.5% freeze chance; executes enemies below a threshold HP. **Unlock:** 50 kills with Frostwalker.
- Complements Ice Cube/Frostwalker builds; pairs with Aura.

### Old Mask
- **Effect:** Not documented on wiki (listed "TBD"). **Unlock:** Found on Graveyard map.

### Skuleg
- **Effect:** +7% Difficulty. **Unlock:** Reach 40% total Difficulty in a single run.
- Trades safety for more XP/gold via increased enemy strength; pairs with Mirror, Oats for risk mitigation.

### Ghost
- **Effect:** Summons ghost allies via an interactable; ghosts get player damage bonuses and trigger on-hit effects. **Unlock:** Survive the Final Swarm for 60 seconds.
- Pairs with Aura, Firestaff for sustained battlefield presence.

### Cactus
- **Effect:** Shoots spikes when taking damage, scaling with Thorns. **Unlock:** Defeat 100 cactus enemies using Thorns damage.
- Pairs with Spiky Shield, Aegis; strong for melee-contact builds.

### Credit Card (Red)
- **Effect:** Permanently +2.5% Damage when opening a chest (stacks per chest opened for the run). **Unlock:** available from start; found in chests/run rewards.
- Pairs with Luck-boosting items/chest-frequency builds.

### Gas Mask
- **Effect:** Gain Armor and Overheal for each poisoned enemy nearby. **Unlock:** 5,000 kills as Amog.
- One of only two Overheal sources in the game (the other is Chonkplate). Pairs with Moldy Cheese, Poison Flask, Toxic Barrel.

## Rare Items

### Demonic Blade
- **Effect:** Crits have a +25% chance to heal 1 HP. First copy also grants +1% Crit Chance (subsequent copies do not). **Unlock:** 350 total HP healed via Lifesteal.
- Pairs with CL4NK, Precision Tome, Forbidden Juice, Grandma's Secret Tonic, Giant Fork. Overpowered Lamp does not add extra healing triggers.

### Demonic Blood
- **Effect:** +0.5 Max HP per kill, capped at 200 stacks (+100 Max HP total). **Unlock:** Increase Max HP 400 times using Blood Magic.
- Pairs with Blood Magic, Vlad, tank builds.

### Echo Shard
- **Effect:** +12% chance to spawn a bonus XP Shard on kill. **Unlock:** Upgrade XP Tome to Level 8.
- Pairs with Time Bracelet, Attraction Tome, Sucky Magnet.

### Moldy Gloves
- **Effect:** Creates a moldy poison cloud on hit (AoE poison spread). **Unlock:** Use a Microwave to duplicate Moldy Cheese 3 times consecutively.
- Pairs with Poison Flask, Amog, Aura, Firestaff.

### Golden Sneakers
- **Effect:** Earn money by moving. **Unlock:** Break 150 pots.
- Pairs with Golden Glove, mobility-focused builds.

### Idle Juice
- **Effect:** Damage ramps up to +100% while standing still. **Unlock:** Kill 500 enemies without moving.
- Pairs with Aura, Frostwalker, Campfire — stationary/control builds. Additively stacks with Gym Sauce/Brass Knuckles (worse scaling together).

### Leeching Crystal
- **Effect:** +50% Max HP, −50% HP Regen (multiplicative — each copy applies x0.5 to Regen). **Unlock:** Upgrade Regen Tome to Level 8.
- **Warning:** 3+ copies push HP Regen negative, dealing self-damage over time (cannot drop below 1 HP; does not affect shields). Negative-regen "damage" ticks trigger Electric Plug, Golden Shield, Cowards Cloak (self-triggered without enemy hits) but not Calcium's passive or Mirror. Lifesteal-based healing (Borgar, Beacon, Demonic Blade, Slurp Gloves) bypasses the regen penalty. Useful for maintaining 1 HP with Corrupted Sword / Holy Book synergy.

### Pumpkin
- **Effect:** Not documented on wiki (listed "TBD"). **Unlock:** As Roberto, break every pumpkin on Graveyard.

### Brass Knuckles
- **Effect:** +20% more damage to nearby enemies. **Unlock:** 5,000 kills with Sword.
- Pairs with Sword, Aegis, Corrupted Sword, Battery, Turbo Socks.

### Unstable Transfusion — see Default section above (listed Rare on its own page).

## Epic Items

### Kevin
- **Effect:** 25% chance to punch you on hit — self-damage that cannot kill you, bypasses shields, and still triggers on-hit effects. **Unlock:** Obtain 3 Leeching Crystal in a single run.
- Enables self-triggered reflection builds (Mirror, Spiky Shield) without needing enemy contact.

### Beefy Ring
- **Effect:** +20% Damage per 100 Max HP. **Unlock:** available by default.
- Pairs with Oats, Leeching Crystal, Demonic Blood, Chonkplate — HP-stacking builds.

### Credit Card (Green)
- **Effect:** Permanently +2% Luck when opening a chest; also +10% Chest price per copy owned. **Unlock:** available from start; found in chests/run rewards.
- Pairs with Clover and chest-farming runs.

### Turbo Skates
- **Effect:** Move Speed converts to Attack Speed (max +40% per stack). **Unlock:** Upgrade both Cooldown Tome AND Agility Tome to Level 5 in the same run.
- Pairs with Turbo Socks, Cowards Cloak.

### Slutty Cannon
- **Effect:** 20% chance to spawn missiles on kill. **Unlock:** 5,000 kills with Slutty Rocket.
- Pairs with Quantity Tome, Damage Tome.

### Shattered Knowledge
- **Effect:** XP Shards slice through enemies while traveling (passive damage). **Unlock:** Upgrade Attraction Tome to Level 8.
- Pairs with Echo Shard, Sucky Magnet; can trigger on-hit effects.

### Quins Mask
- **Effect:** Thorns damage has a 50% chance to explode. **Unlock:** As Athena, deliver the final blow to a Stage Boss while wielding Aegis.
- Pairs with Spiky Shield, Cactus; scales with Armor and Thorns.

### Toxic Barrel
- **Effect:** Releases a toxic cloud when hit — applies 10 stacks of poison to every enemy struck by the cloud. **Unlock:** 2,000 kills via poison damage.
- Pairs with Moldy Gloves, Poison Flask, Gas Mask.

### Gamer Goggles
- **Effect:** Increases damage the lower your current HP is, up to +97% at 1 HP. **Unlock:** Defeat a Stage Boss below 10% HP.
- Stacks additively with most common boosters (suboptimal); stacks multiplicatively with Gym Sauce, Idle Juice, Brass Knuckles, Speed Boi (better). Pairs with Corrupted Sword, Cursed Grabbies, Mirror, Kevin (to sustain low-HP state safely).

### Eagle Claw
- **Effect:** +66% Damage vs airborne enemies, 8% chance to launch enemies airborne. **Unlock:** 10,000 kills as Birdo.
- Pairs with Spiky Shield, Aegis, Bananarang.

### Demonic Soul
- **Effect:** +0.1% Damage per kill, capped at +100%. Wiki calls it "one of the strongest scaling items in the game." **Unlock:** Reach Level 30 as Calcium.
- Pairs with Aura, Firestaff, and any high-kill-count build.

### Bob (Dead)
- **Effect:** For every 14 units moved, summons "Bob (dead)" — a passive damage-dealing companion. **Unlock:** Survive the Final Swarm for 2 minutes.
- Pairs with Turbo Socks, Cowards Cloak for frequent summons.

### Grandmas Secret Tonic
- **Effect:** Crits have a 50% chance to explode, dealing 50% damage to nearby enemies. Explosion radius: 4m base, up to 8m max. First copy grants +2% Crit Chance (additional copies don't); radius grows +1m per extra copy (scales with Size — at 2x Size, one copy maxes radius; at 1x Size, 5 copies needed). **Unlock:** 3,000 kills with Sniper Rifle.
- Pairs with CL4NK, Precision Tome, Forbidden Juice, Giant Fork. Overpowered Lamp DOES boost this item's explosion trigger (unlike most crit items). Wiki tip: Banish excess copies beyond 5 (no further radius benefit).

### Cursed Grabbies
- **Effect:** +10% Difficulty, 0.8x Max HP, 5% on-hit chance to cast curse. **Unlock:** Upgrade Cursed Tome to Level 10.
- High-risk item; pairs with Cursed Doll, Corrupted Sword.

### Bob's Light
- **Effect:** A sphere of light explodes every 45s, killing all nearby enemies; stacking reduces cooldown by 3s and increases radius by 10m per copy. **Unlock:** Defeat Big Bob in Graveyard with the finishing blow dealt by a lamp's damage.
- Strategy section marked "TBD" on the wiki (incomplete documentation).

### Gas Mask — see Common section above.

### Chonkplate
- **Effect:** +20% Lifesteal; allows Overheal up to 75% of Max HP. **Unlock:** Reach 500 Max HP.
- One of two Overheal sources (other is Gas Mask). Pairs with Leeching Crystal, Bloody Cleaver, Demonic Blood.

## Legendary Items

### Overpowered Lamp — see Default section above.

### Za Warudo — see Default section above.

### Big Bonk — see Default section above.

### Spicy Meatball
- **Effect:** Attacks have a 25% chance to explode, dealing 65% damage to surrounding enemies. Explosion radius: 4m base, up to 8m max (same scaling rules as Grandma's Secret Tonic — Size stat and copy count affect radius; 5 copies max useful at 1x Size). **Unlock:** available from start.
- Explosion damage scales from the triggering hit's damage (not overall Damage stat) — viable on most characters/weapons. Poor with Poison Flask (low base damage; poison doesn't trigger explosions).

### Ice Cube
- **Effect:** 20% chance to inflict ice damage (can freeze) on hit. Acquisition method not specified on source page.
- Pairs with rapid-attack/spread weapons; useful vs. aggressive elites.

### Giant Fork — see Default section above.

### Lightning Orb
- **Effect:** 25% chance on hit to summon lightning that can stun enemies. **Unlock:** 3,000 kills via lightning damage.
- Pairs with Lightning Staff, Thunder Mitts, Battery, Cooldown Tome.

### Dragonfire
- **Effect:** 15% chance to summon dragonfire on hit; fire damage can now apply Burn. **Unlock:** 4,000 kills via fire damage.
- Pairs with Firestaff, Flamewalker, Dragon's Breath, Spicy Meatball.

### Sucky Magnet
- **Effect:** Periodically pulls in all XP gems on screen. **Unlock:** Complete every Charge Shrine on the map in a single run.
- Strong synergy with Shattered Knowledge (pulled gems become damaging projectiles across the screen).

### Holy Book
- **Effect:** +100 Max HP, +50 HP Regen; healing radiates damaging energy to nearby enemies. **Unlock:** Defeat the first Stage Boss without taking any damage.
- Pairs with defensive/regen-focused builds.

### Speed Boi
- **Effect:** On taking damage below 50% Max HP: slow time and deal 2x damage for 10s (17s cooldown). Additional copies: +2s duration, +1s cooldown per copy (damage multiplier does not stack). **Unlock:** Finish a stage while maintaining at least 2 Boss Curses simultaneously.
- Multiplicative with other damage bonuses. Time slowdown also slows stage/run timers during activation. Pairs with Kevin for safe, reliable triggering.

### Energy Core
- **Effect:** Cooldown reduction and energy regeneration. **Unlock:** Defeat a Final Boss twice.
- Pairs with Cooldown Tome and shrine-focused strategies.

### Anvil
- **Effect:** Weapon upgrade offers gain +1 to their stat value (e.g. a +5% roll becomes +6%), and level-ups may offer 3 choices instead of 2. **Unlock:** Complete 3 Challenges.
- Considered one of the strongest scaling items; best acquired as early as possible.

### Soul Harvester
- **Effect:** Killing an enemy summons two homing souls that attack other enemies. Soul base damage: 5, +1.75 per player level, scales with player Damage stat. **Unlock:** Survive the Final Swarm for 6 minutes.
- Pairs with Aura, Firestaff, Revolver in dense enemy scenarios.

### Joes Dagger
- **Effect:** 1% chance to instantly Execute an enemy (kills normal enemies outright; deals heavy damage to bosses). Each Execute grants a permanent +1% Damage for the run (capped at 200 Executes/minute). Additional copies increase damage-per-Execute by +1% each (Execute chance itself stays fixed). **Unlock:** 10,000 kills as/with Dexecutioner.
- Scales with Attack Speed, Projectile Count, Size (more hits = more Execute rolls). Stacks additively with Beefy Ring/Demonic Soul, multiplicatively with Gym Sauce/Brass Knuckles. Crit Chance/Damage multiply with Execute damage.

### Bloody Cleaver
- **Effect:** Lifesteal triggers also apply Bloodmark stacks, with a 50% chance to apply. **Unlock:** unconfirmed on wiki as of this snapshot.
- Pairs with Chonkplate (high lifesteal) and Unstable Transfusion (Bloodmark synergy).

### Snek
- **Effect:** Applies +4 stacks of poison on hit, with a 5% chance to instantly burst all poison damage. **Unlock:** Found in a concealed chest in the Graveyard crypt.
- Strong scaling with high Attack Speed / high projectile-count builds.

### Pot (Stainless Steel)
- **Effect:** +10 max levels for all weapons; each additional stack gives one less bonus level, down to a minimum of +2 at 9 stacks. **Unlock:** Collect every pot and chest in the first Graveyard crypt.
- Strategy section marked "TBD" on the wiki.

### Wizard's Hat
- **Effect:** Functions like the Stainless Steel Pot but for Tomes (raises Tome max level cap). **Unlock:** Play as Vlad and use a Stainless Steel Pot to upgrade any weapon beyond the standard level-40 cap.
- Strategy section marked "TBD" on the wiki.

### Golden Ring — see Default section above (no known effect).
