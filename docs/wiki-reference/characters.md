# Megabonk Wiki Reference — Characters

Source: https://megabonk.wiki/wiki/Characters (community wiki, MediaWiki)
Snapshot date: 2026-08-17

This is a point-in-time snapshot of community-maintained wiki content. It is **not official game documentation** and may drift from actual current game behavior/balance as patches are released. Use as a reference/hint source only — verify against live event data where possible.

## Index Overview

Players start with **Fox** and **Sir Oofie**. Up to 20 additional characters can be unlocked through in-game conditions (quests, boss kills, challenge completion, etc.). After meeting the unlock requirement, a character must be purchased from the **Unlocks** tab in the Main Menu before becoming available to select.

Total characters covered below: 21.

---

## Fox

- **Starting weapon:** Firestaff
- **Unlock requirement:** Available by default (starting character)
- **Passive ("RNG Blessing"):** Gain 2% Luck per level
- **Role:** Ranged DPS / Lucky
- **Base stats:** Evasion 6% (+5%/level); Luck 15% (+15%/level); Pickup Range 9 (+4/level); Jump Height 8 (+1/level)
- **Notes:** Synergizes with crit chance and projectile-based builds; recommended to invest in Damage Tomes and Luck-scaling items. Trivia: appearance references developer Vedinad's fox-wizard social media avatar.

## Sir Oofie

- **Starting weapon:** Sword
- **Unlock requirement:** Available by default (starting character)
- **Passive ("Reinforced"):** Gain 1% Armor per level
- **Role:** Tank / Melee frontline
- **Base stats:** Max HP 150 (+50/level); Armor 17% (+17%/level)
- **Notes:** "Slow, but his armor makes him pretty chunky." Synergizes with Thorns Tome and sustained-contact items; pairs well with crowd-control/AoE to offset limited melee range.

## Calcium

- **Starting weapon:** Bone
- **Unlock requirement:** Defeat 1,000 Skeletons (cumulative across runs), then unlock via Main Menu → Unlocks tab
- **Passive ("Speed Demon"):** Speed builds up over time, but is halved when you take damage. Gain damage based on speed.
- **Role:** Ranged / Skirmisher
- **Base stats:** Move Speed 1.2x (+0.2x/level); Pickup Range 8 (+3/level); Jump Height 9 (+2/level)
- **Notes:** Risk-reward dynamic — avoiding damage sustains the speed/damage bonus stack.

## Megachad

- **Starting weapon:** Aura
- **Unlock requirement:** Upgrade Damage Tome to Level 7 in a run, then unlock via Unlocks tab
- **Passive ("Flex"):** Stuns enemies and prevents damage; cooldown decreases 0.2s per level. Gain 2.5% damage when you flex.
- **Role:** Tank / Crowd Control
- **Base stats:** Max HP 120 (+20/level); Move Speed 8 (+1/level)
- **Notes:** Cooldown reduction items synergize with Flex's ability-level scaling.

## Ogre

- **Starting weapon:** Axe
- **Unlock requirement:** Defeat 15,000 Goblins (cumulative), then purchase from Unlocks tab
- **Passive ("Warrior"):** Gain 1.5% damage per level
- **Role:** Bruiser
- **Base stats:** Max HP 140 (+40/level); Armor 6% (+6%/level); Move Speed 0.9x (−0.1x/level); Luck −1% (−1%/level)
- **Notes:** "Critical thinking skills of a wet sock." High offensive scaling; benefits from AoE upgrades and melee synergies; high aggro draw recommends defensive investment.

## CL4NK

- **Starting weapon:** Revolver
- **Unlock requirement:** Complete Forest Tier 1, then unlock via Unlocks tab
- **Passive ("Crit Happens"):** Gain 1% Crit Chance per level
- **Role:** Ranged / Glass Cannon
- **Base stats:** Max HP 40 (−60 relative to baseline); Shield 50 (+50); Move Speed 1.1x (+0.1x); Pickup Range 8 (+3); Jump Height 9 (+2)
- **Special mechanic:** Teleporting to the second stage of Forest Tier 2 while playing CL4NK unlocks the Desert map.
- **Notes:** Crit/single-target burst focus; fragile early game.

## Athena

- **Starting weapon:** Aegis
- **Unlock requirement:** Upgrade Thorns Tome to Level 9, then unlock via Unlocks tab
- **Passive:** Deals more damage at lower HP; gains +2 Thorns per level; attack speed +150% when no shields are active
- **Role:** Tank
- **Base stats:** Max HP 125 (+25/level); Armor 17% (+17%/level); Thorns 5 (+5/level)
- **Notes:** Built around low-HP risk/reward with Thorns/Armor synergy.

## Robinette

- **Starting weapon:** Bow
- **Unlock requirement:** Complete Forest Tier 2, then purchase from Unlocks tab
- **Passive ("Stonks"):** +1% Gold Gain per level. Increase Damage by 10% per 1,000 gold held in inventory (diminishing returns beyond 200k gold).
- **Role:** Mobile ranged scout
- **Base stats:** Move Speed 1.1x (+0.1x/level); Pickup Range 8 (+3/level); Jump Height 10 (+3/level)
- **Notes:** Incentivizes gold accumulation to scale damage.

## Monke

- **Starting weapon:** Bananarang
- **Unlock requirement:** Find both the Bananarang and a Gold Key in the Forest, use the key to unlock Monke's cage, then activate unlock via Main Menu
- **Passive ("Monke Grip"):** Can climb walls; +2 Max HP per level
- **Role:** Brawler / High HP
- **Base stats:** Max HP 135 (+35/level); Damage 1.2x (+0.2x/level); Jump Height 9 (+2/level)
- **Notes:** Wall-climbing enables unique traversal/positioning.

## Tony McZoom

- **Starting weapon:** Wireless Dagger
- **Unlock requirement:** Complete 2 Challenges, then purchase from Unlocks tab
- **Passive ("Zap"):** Charge an electric attack by moving around. Gain 2% Pickup Range per level.
- **Role:** Speed / Assassin
- **Notes:** Immune to fall damage. Built for fast movement and burst damage; synergizes with Speed and Crit Chance. Specific stat values not listed on source page.

## Bush

- **Starting weapon:** Sniper Rifle
- **Unlock requirement:** Locate and defeat Bush as a boss encounter in Forest, then unlock via Unlocks tab
- **Passive ("Bullseye"):** Random enemies are marked with a crosshair; shooting them causes an explosion around them. +1% Crit Damage per level.
- **Role:** Ranged
- **Base stats:** Evasion 10% (+9%/level); Move Speed 11 (+6/level)
- **Notes:** Long-range engagement; synergizes with crit chance/ranged bonuses; best played at distance.

## Birdo

- **Starting weapon:** Tornado
- **Unlock requirement:** Defeat 100 enemies using the Tornado weapon while caught in a tornado in the Desert biome
- **Passive:** Hold "Jump" to float in the air. Gain 1% Airborne Damage per level.
- **Role:** Mobile
- **Base stats:** Move Speed 1.1x (+0.1x/level); Pickup Range 10 (+5); Jump Height 9 (+2); Extra Jumps 1 (+1)
- **Notes:** Immune to fall damage. Excels at crowd control via tornado AoE and positioning tactics.

## Noelle

- **Starting weapon:** Frostwalker
- **Unlock requirement:** Use a Microwave to duplicate an Ice Crystal 3 times consecutively in one run, then unlock via Unlocks tab
- **Passive:** Gain 0.75% Size per level. Increase Damage by 1.5% per currently frozen enemy.
- **Role:** Mage / Control
- **Notes:** Ice-based crowd control focus; synergizes with survivability items and freeze mechanics.

## Amog

- **Starting weapon:** Poison Flask
- **Unlock requirement:** Poison 50,000 enemies using Moldy Cheese, then obtain the Poison Flask
- **Passive ("Plague"):** Poisoned enemies explode on death, spreading all poison stacks to enemies hit. Gain 1% Poison Damage per level.
- **Role:** Crowd Control
- **Base stats:** Max HP 130 (+30/level); Armor 12% (+12%/level); Pickup Range 10 (+5/level)
- **Notes:** Poison damage scales off the Damage multiplier but does NOT benefit from critical strikes. Poison spread radius scales with Size stat; duration affects total spread damage output.

## Spaceman

- **Starting weapon:** Black Hole
- **Unlock requirement:** Complete 6 Challenges, then purchase from Unlocks tab
- **Passive ("Quantum XP Theory"):** Gain 1% XP Gain per level
- **Role:** Crowd Control
- **Notes:** Immune to fall damage. Area control/gravitational focus; synergizes with cooldown and projectile upgrades to maximize Black Hole uptime.

## Bandit

- **Starting weapon:** Dexecutioner
- **Unlock requirement:** Locate and defeat Bandit in the Desert, then unlock via Unlocks tab
- **Passive ("Flowstate"):** +1% Attack Speed per level
- **Role:** Melee / Assassin
- **Base stats:** Evasion 10% (+9%/level); Move Speed 1.1x (+0.1x/level); Pickup Range 9 (+4/level); Jump Height 8 (+1/level)
- **Notes:** Fast-paced close-range combat; synergizes with crit chance and melee damage amplification.

## Ninja

- **Starting weapon:** Katana
- **Unlock requirement:** Complete Desert Tier 1, then unlock via Unlocks tab
- **Passive ("Shadowstep"):** Executes enemies after evasion; gains +0.5% Evasion per level
- **Role:** Melee / Assassin
- **Base stats:** Max HP 6% (+5%/level, likely a relative modifier); Move Speed 1.2x (+0.2x/level); Pickup Range 8 (+3/level); Jump Height 9 (+2/level)
- **Notes:** Dodge-focused build; synergizes with Speed, Evasion, and Crit Damage; execute mechanic ties into the Evasion stat (see stats.md).

## Vlad

- **Starting weapon:** Blood Magic
- **Unlock requirement:** Complete Desert Tier 2 AND have Blood Magic unlocked, then unlock via Unlocks tab
- **Passive ("Vampire"):** +1% Lifesteal per level
- **Role:** Mage / Lifesteal
- **Notes:** Sustain-focused via HP drain; pairs with lifesteal/spell-damage tomes and items. Flavor text: "He looks pretty evil but he's actually a really nice dude."

## Sir Chadwell

- **Starting weapon:** Corrupted Sword
- **Unlock requirement:** Complete Forest Tier 3, then unlock via Unlocks tab
- **Passive (Curse):** Gain 1% Difficulty per level
- **Role:** Melee
- **Notes:** Corrupted Sword's damage multiplier is HP-based — damage decreases at higher HP and peaks at very low HP (high-risk/high-reward). The Difficulty-scaling passive makes enemies progressively stronger/faster/more frequent as Chadwell levels, escalating challenge on long runs.

## Dicehead

- **Starting weapon:** Dice
- **Unlock requirement:** Complete 100 Quests, then unlock via Unlocks tab
- **Passive ("Gamba"):** Roll the dice and upgrade a random stat when you level up
- **Role:** RNG / Trickster
- **Notes:** Designed for randomness/risk-taking; scales well with luck-based mechanics on long runs. Specific stat values not listed on source page.

## Roberto

- **Starting weapon:** Scythe
- **Unlock requirement:** Defeat Spooky Steve (Graveyard boss)
- **Passive ("Hoarder"):** Free chest every 60s, increasing to 120s over levels. Can also float slightly.
- **Role:** listed as "TBA" on wiki (not yet categorized)
- **Notes:** Flavor text: "He claims to be floating. Scientists and experts are still investigating." Detailed stat values not provided on source page.
