This is a point-in-time snapshot of community-maintained wiki content. It is **not official game documentation** and may drift from actual current game behavior/balance as patches are released. Use as a reference/hint source only — verify against live event data where possible.

## Index Overview

Tomes are passive upgrades providing permanent stat bonuses for the duration of a run. Each tome upgrades up to **Level 99**, with per-level effect magnitude scaling by the rarity tier of the upgrade offered (Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary). Reaching over 100% Crit Chance (via Precision Tome or other sources) enables "Overcrit" — additional crit-damage applications.

13 default (start-unlocked) tomes, 10 unlockable tomes. Total: 23.

## Default Tomes (13) — unlocked from game start

### Agility Tome
- **Effect:** Increases overall movement speed.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common +15%, Uncommon +18%, Rare +21%, Epic +24%, Legendary +30%
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.

### Cooldown Tome
- **Effect:** Reduces cooldown between attacks (attack speed increase across all weapons).
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 8%, Uncommon 9%, Rare 11%, Epic 12%, Legendary 15%
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.

### Damage Tome
- **Effect:** Increases base damage multiplier, scaling with most weapons.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common +0.08x, Uncommon +0.1x, Rare +0.11x, Epic +0.13x, Legendary +0.16x
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.

### Evasion Tome
- **Effect:** Increases chance to completely avoid incoming attacks (no damage taken on trigger).
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common +10%, Uncommon +12%, Rare +14%, Epic +16%, Legendary +20%
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.
- **Note:** Evasion has diminishing returns; caps around ~94% effectiveness (matches the general Evasion stat cap described in stats.md).

### Golden Tome
- **Effect:** Increases Gold earned from all sources.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 12%, Uncommon 14%, Rare 17%, Epic 19%, Legendary 24%
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.

### Health Tome
- **Effect:** Increases Max HP.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common +25, Uncommon +30, Rare +35, Epic +40, Legendary +50
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.

### Knockback Tome
- **Effect:** Increases distance enemies are pushed back on hit; helps melee create space.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 20%, Uncommon 24%, Rare 28%, Epic 32%, Legendary 40%
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.

### Precision Tome
- **Effect:** Increases Crit Chance; enables Overcrit above 100%.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 7%, Uncommon 8%, Rare 10%, Epic 11%, Legendary 14%
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.

### Projectile Speed Tome
- **Effect:** Increases projectile velocity (faster hits, longer effective range before despawn).
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 15%, Uncommon 18%, Rare 21%, Epic 24%, Legendary 30%
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.
- **Note:** Especially benefits Ghost, Slutty Cannon, Soul Harvester items (per wiki).

### Regen Tome
- **Effect:** Increases HP Regen (per-minute passive healing); stat value = HP regenerated per minute.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 40, Uncommon 48, Rare 56, Epic 64, Legendary 80
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.
- **Note:** Bleed status (from Bark Vader / Juge Anubis) disables Regen; Poison does not.

### Shield Tome
- **Effect:** Grants a shield that absorbs damage before HP; regenerates after a short period without damage taken.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 25, Uncommon 30, Rare 35, Epic 40, Legendary 50
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.
- **Note:** Poison and bleed effects bypass the shield and hit HP directly.

### Silver Tome
- **Effect:** Increases Silver earned from all sources (does not affect Gold or XP).
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 12%, Uncommon 14%, Rare 17%, Epic 19%, Legendary 24%
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.

### Size Tome
- **Effect:** Increases size of attacks, projectiles, and explosions.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common +10%, Uncommon +12%, Rare +14%, Epic +16%, Legendary +20%
- **Max level:** 99. **Unlock:** available at start.

## Unlockable Tomes (10)

### Armor Tome
- **Effect:** Increases Armor (damage reduction %); diminishing returns, caps ~94%.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common +12%, Uncommon +14%, Rare +17%, Epic +19%, Legendary +24%
- **Unlock:** Kill 5,000 enemies as Sir Oofie.

### Attraction Tome
- **Effect:** Increases pickup range (XP orb collection distance).
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 75%, Uncommon 90%, Rare 105%, Epic 120%, Legendary 150%
- **Unlock:** Use a Shrine 8 times.

### Bloody Tome
- **Effect:** Chance to heal 1 HP on hit (lifesteal); above 100% chance, healing is guaranteed with a chance for a second HP point.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 10%, Uncommon 12%, Rare 14%, Epic 16%, Legendary 20%
- **Unlock:** Kill 12,500 enemies.
- **Note:** Disabled by Bleed (Bark Vader, Juge Anubis); unaffected by Poison.

### Chaos Tome
- **Effect:** Grants a random stat boost per level (reward bonus +50% as of patch 1.0.12).
- **Per-level bonus:** randomized stat category each level; no fixed numeric table given.
- **Unlock:** Charge all Charge Shrines on a Tier 3 run across all 3 stages without leaving the charge zone.
- **Known bug (per wiki, as of patch 1.0.19):** Legendary-tier upgrades reportedly grant double the intended bonus.

### Cursed Tome
- **Effect:** Increases enemy spawn count/HP/speed/damage in exchange for more XP and drops (Difficulty stat category).
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 4%, Uncommon 4%, Rare 5%, Epic 6%, Legendary 7% (wiki notes a discrepancy: base upgrade listed as "+3.5%" but implementation appears to be 4% at Common)
- **Unlock:** Beat a Stage Boss in under 5 minutes.

### Duration Tome
- **Effect:** Increases how long projectiles/attack effects/debuffs persist.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common +15%, Uncommon +18%, Rare +21%, Epic +24%, Legendary +30%
- **Unlock:** Upgrade Axe to level 10.

### Luck Tome
- **Effect:** Increases Luck — improves tier quality of level-up offers, shrine rolls, and chest rewards.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 7%, Uncommon 8%, Rare 10%, Epic 11%, Legendary 14%
- **Unlock:** 0.01% random drop chance from enemies (very rare).
- **Note:** Shady Guy shop inventory is rolled on stage entry, so Luck does not affect it retroactively.

### Quantity Tome
- **Effect:** Increases projectile/attack count depending on weapon. Does NOT affect Aura, Frostwalker, or Dragon's Breath.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 1, Uncommon 1.2, Rare 1.4, Epic 1.6, Legendary 2 (additional projectiles)
- **Unlock:** Fire 5,000 projectiles (occurs naturally through ranged play).

### Thorns Tome
- **Effect:** Reflects damage back to enemies when they hit you.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common +15, Uncommon +18, Rare +21, Epic +24, Legendary +30
- **Unlock:** Block 250 attacks with Aegis.
- **Note (as of v1.0.19):** Thorns retaliation cannot trigger while the Shield Powerup effect is active.

### XP Tome
- **Effect:** Increases XP gained from all sources.
- **Per-level bonus by rarity:** Common 7%, Uncommon 8%, Rare 10%, Epic 11%, Legendary 14%
- **Unlock:** Break 20 pots in a single run.

---

All tomes share: Max Level 99, five-tier rarity scaling (Common/Uncommon/Rare/Epic/Legendary) determining per-level magnitude.
