## Overview

The Shop is a permanent meta-progression system accessed from the Main Menu. Players spend **Silver** (a persistent currency, separate from in-run Gold) to purchase upgrades that apply to **all future runs**. Individual upgrades unlock at specific cumulative-quest-completion thresholds (20-35+ quests depending on the upgrade).

## Shop Upgrades

### Silver
- **Effect:** Increases the amount of Silver gained during runs.
- **Unlock:** Clear Tier 1 on any map once.
- **Cost progression (Silver per point):** 18 → 25 → 32 → 40 → 49 (first two values not confirmed by wiki source; general range given elsewhere as 18-49).

### Refresh
- **Effect:** Adds extra refreshes available during level-up choices.
- **Unlock:** Complete 20 total quests.
- **Cost progression:** 8 → 15 → 24 → 33.

### Weapon Slots
- **Effect:** Expands weapon inventory capacity (equip more weapons per run).
- **Unlock:** Complete 25 total quests.
- **Cost progression:** 25 → 125 range noted; first major slot cited elsewhere at 50. Exact intermediate steps not fully itemized by the wiki.
- **Note:** "Strongly recommended for Tier 2 and higher" per the index page. Additional Weapon Slot 1 = complete 25 total quests; Additional Weapon Slot 2 = complete 45 total quests (per Quests page).

### Skip
- **Effect:** Adds extra skips available during level-up choices.
- **Unlock:** Complete 30 total quests.
- **Cost progression:** 8 → 15 → 24 → 33 → 43 → 55.

### Tome Slots
- **Effect:** Expands tome inventory capacity (equip more tomes per run).
- **Unlock:** Complete 35 total quests.
- **Cost progression:** 35 → 135 range noted; intermediate steps not fully itemized by the wiki.
- **Note:** "Strongly recommended for Tier 2 and higher." Additional Tome Slot 1 = complete 35 total quests; Additional Tome Slot 2 = complete 55 total quests (per Quests page).

### Boombox
- **Effect:** Unlocks in-run soundtrack selection before starting a run.
- **Unlock:** Find and activate all boomboxes on the Forest map.
- **Cost:** 5 Silver.

### Toggler
- **Effect:** Unlocked after purchasing a threshold number of other Shop upgrades (per wiki: "unlocks a special feature" — exact functional description not detailed on source page).
- **Unlock:** Purchase 40 total Shop upgrades.
- **Cost:** 50 Silver.

## Related Shop-adjacent unlocks (from Quests page, see quests.md)
- **Banish** (Shop) — Complete 40 total quests.

All Shop upgrades are permanent and apply across all future runs (not just the current one).
