## Overview

Megabonk's quest system consists of hidden objectives rather than a visible quest log/tracker. Quests fall into four informal categories per the wiki: Milestone, Mastery & Performance, Exploration & Interaction, and Specific Loadout. Completing quests counts toward cumulative "total quests completed" thresholds that gate several Shop upgrades (see shop.md) and some character unlocks.

## Skin Unlock Quests (standardized pattern across characters)

| Skin | Requirement | Silver Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Black / Alternate | Kill 15,000 enemies | 1 Silver |
| Light / White | Defeat first Stage Boss in under 6 minutes | 4 Silver |
| Fire | Kill the Final Boss on Tier 3 | 4 Silver |
| Gold | Character reaches Rank 100 | 4 Silver |
| Red / Blue* | Kill 50,000 enemies | 2 Silver |
| White / Pink* | Kill 100,000 enemies | 2 Silver |

\* Only available for characters with 6+ total skins (per wiki: Dicehead has 6 total; Amog and Tony McZoom have 7 total).
